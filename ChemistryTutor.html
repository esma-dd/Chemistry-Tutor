About Me

Hello! I'm Esma Dede. I am graduated from Boğaziçi University and I have BS degree in chemistry.

I have tutored in several chemistry topics online in English and Turkish using software. I used office programs and chemistry software to deliver lectures. I can easily draw chemical structures, diagrams and write scientific/mathematical symbols to illustrate better and to enhance the quality of learning experience.

A problem with learning which is frequently mentioned among students is that "what is the use of it(school knowledge)?". I apply a method to cope with this problem. traditional method of teaching is to deliver topic first then to solve exercise questions. The method I use is that to explore questions directly, to highlight key concepts and to learn how to use knowledge in action.

As a tutor, I can help students actively learn by constantly asking them questions, encouraging them to participate in problem solving and critical thinking. I want to make students acquire ability of critical, analytical and creative thinking, problem solving.

I have gotten experience with college level chemistry topics and exams. I do not have an experience of specific placement exam e.g. AP, IB, A-Level however I am confident that I can adapt myself to any sort of chemistry examination and support students to accomplish their goals.

In a classroom one teacher can not possibly take care of each student equally. However a student can receive intensive care in one to one tuition. An online education enables that students meet the best teachers all around the world.

I would like to mention my social skills. I am amiable and compassionate towards students. If a student do not understand a topic I respond being patient and encouraging. I additionally observe students' learning process so that I can underline challenging topics further.

Feel free to contact me anytime to ask about my tutoring services, arrange date and time of lecture session.
